[revision decisions 2026-10-02: P3a, verified against the FULL TEXTS, not the abstracts]
  alves_kan_2026: arXiv 2607.15525v1 (17 Jul 2026), Felippe Alves and Renato Vicente; PDF downloaded from arxiv.org/pdf/2607.15525 and read.
    - Section "Control analysis: random initialization versus trained edges": the trained edge-function audit of a grid-2 B-spline KAN is compared with "a randomly initialized (untrained) grid-2 KAN and ... an initialization-variance sweep".
    - Top-four fPCA variance "is not diagnostic of training at grid 2": a freshly initialised grid-2 KAN already gives 99.9%. They call it "basis-imposed".
    - Closed-form approximability is training-induced: 93-97% top-50 coverage when trained, against 63% for matched random-init grid-2 edges.
    - Table 9 repeats the random-initialised control on BabyLM.
    - The abstract does not mention the control.
    - Supports: "an untrained random-initialisation control has shown one edge-function metric to be imposed by the basis rather than learned". The models are B-spline KANs, not wavelet KANs.
  mysore_temporal_2026: arXiv 2605.05685v1 (7 May 2026), Naveen Mysore; PDF downloaded from arxiv.org/pdf/2605.05685 and read.
    - Appendix, "Faithfulness sanity checks": "Following Adebayo et al. [2018], deletion ranking is tested after randomizing model weights."
    - On Weather, top-50 deletion MSE is +2.20e-4 for the trained model, 0.0 with all KAN weights randomised, and +7.4e-5 with only the spline coefficients randomised.
    - A random-deletion baseline is "consistent with zero (-0.01 +- 0.05 x 10^-4, 50 draws)".
    - The edge functions are B-splines.
    - The abstract does not mention the randomisation check.
    - Supports: "a weight-randomisation check has been applied to KAN attributions".
  Both DOIs (10.48550/arXiv.2607.15525, 10.48550/arXiv.2605.05685) resolve to the arXiv records. Neither paper uses a wavelet KAN or ECG data, so the manuscript's "first test of the wavelet-KAN interpretability premise against explicit null models" remains accurate as worded.
