# `results/` — what is current, what is superseded

This directory holds artifacts from the whole life of the project, not only the
experiments the manuscript reports. That is deliberate (nothing is deleted), but
it means a reader needs a map. **Only the directories under "Current" below
back numbers in the paper.** Everything under "Superseded" is retained as a
record of earlier exploration and is not referenced by any published result.

The manuscript is `Submission_Array/manuscript.tex` (updated 2026-09-30; the
earlier `Submission_JBHI/ieee_manuscript_v2.tex` is superseded). Every
quantitative claim in it is re-derived from the files here by
`python src/verify_manuscript_numbers.py`, which recomputes the statistics from
the per-seed records rather than trusting any stored summary.

### Artifacts intentionally not published

Raw per-seed prediction arrays (`*predictions.npy`, `*true.npy`, `*probs.npy`)
total ~215 MB and are omitted. Nothing depends on them: the per-seed confusion
matrices are published, and `per_class_metrics.json` stores the per-seed
TP/FP/FN counts. They can be regenerated by re-running evaluation from the
published checkpoints.

### One superseded file that can mislead

`was_multiseed_summary.json` and `was_scores.json` report the **old
group-mean** alignment score (≈0.958), which is *not* the number in the paper.
That aggregation averages each channel block's mean parameter, and per-edge drift
largely cancels under it: in the trained model, the mean per-edge |Δμ| is 0.064,
against 0.004 for the deviation of the block means. The 0.002 figure previously
quoted here was never verified. The manuscript reports the per-edge Parameter-space
Prior Retention (PPR) instead: trained 0.783, untrained-PCWI 0.973, untrained
isotropic 0.670, trained isotropic 0.579.
